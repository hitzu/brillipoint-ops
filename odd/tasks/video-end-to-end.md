## Specs

Fuente: Issue brillipoint-ops#3 (el Issue manda sobre Engram). Citas literales.

- S1. "**PB is the flag.** Video is enabled by the operator in PB (`VideoBetaSettings.enabled`, device-local, off by default […]). There is **no** video flag in the backend or the front. PB only requests video upload URLs when video is on."
- S2. "**Video is fully independent from photos.** It does not count toward `photoCount` or the session photo quota. If PB never uploads a video, nothing changes for anyone: photo responses keep their exact current shape and the front shows no trace of video."
- S3. "**A video is never a gallery cover.** Videos are ordered last among the session items, after all photos."
- S4. "**Isolated and additive API** so video can be removed without photos noticing (no breaking changes until video proves it sells): own table, own endpoints, optional read field. `Photo` is not touched."
- S5. "**Same storage bucket**, in a `videos` subfolder next to `minimized` (`photobooth/${eventId}/minimized/...`, `sessions.service.ts:484`): `photobooth/${eventId}/videos/${fileId}.mp4` plus its poster `.jpg`. For now a session has at most one video."
- S7. "The API does not validate whether video is "enabled" for an event (PB is the gate). Accepted MVP risk […]"
- S8. "**Own storage**: a dedicated table (e.g. `session_videos`: session, storage path, poster path, mime, duration)."
- S9. "**Own endpoints**: dedicated video presign/confirm (e.g. under `/sessions/:token/videos`) returning upload URLs for `photobooth/${eventId}/videos/${fileId}.mp4` and its poster `.jpg`. Photo presign/confirm stays untouched."
- S10. "**Optional read field**: session (and gallery session) responses gain an optional `videos` field, present only when the session has videos. Photo items keep their exact current shape."
- S11. "Photo endpoints and the `Photo` table keep their exact current behavior (JPEG-only, same request/response shape); removing the video table and endpoints would not affect photos."
- S12 (supuesto, no pedido): el contrato PB↔API cambia; "update `system.yaml` and contracts when declared" (Riesgos del Issue). No existe `system.yaml` hoy: se anota, no se inventa.

Fuera de esta corrida: S6 (offline en PB), front (Fase 3) y subida desde PB (Fase 4), pendientes humanos T4/T5/T6 de PB.
